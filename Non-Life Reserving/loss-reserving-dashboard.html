Exhibit 01

Chain Ladder Method

Projects future claim payments by applying age-to-age loss development factors, derived from the average historical progression between successive development periods, to the latest diagonal of paid losses.

Development formula Ci,k+1 = Ci,k × fk
where fk = ΣCi,k+1 / ΣCi,k

Exhibit 02

London Chain Method

A regression-based variant of the chain ladder: for each development period, cumulative losses at k+1 are fitted as a linear function of losses at k, allowing an intercept term to absorb a fixed cost component that a proportional (multiplicative) factor alone cannot capture.

Development formula Ci,k+1 = ak + bk × Ci,k
where ak, bk = OLS intercept & slope

Exhibit 03

Taylor Separation Method

Decomposes incremental payments on the calendar-year diagonal into a settlement pattern r(j), fixed by development age, and an inflation / claims-cost index λ(t), fixed by calendar period — separating volume from monetary trend before projecting the future square.

Separation formula Xi,j = r(j) × λ(i+j)
fitted trend ln λ(t) = α + βt
